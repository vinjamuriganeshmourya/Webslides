"""The hook: Anjali cannot find the apology, and Sanjay swears he sent it.

Two weeks together, Sanjay forgot, and Anjali says he never even apologised.
Sanjay says he did, on Tuesday. She searches the chat for `sorry`. **No
messages found.** So one of them is lying, and the room gets to decide which.

Neither. Scroll up and it is right there, from Tuesday: `S O R R Y`, typed
with a space after every letter the way people do when they mean it. The
search compares characters, and `sorry` is not inside `S O R R Y`.

That single screen carries both halves of the session. A space is a character,
which is why two phone numbers that look identical are not equal forty minutes
later; and the letters you want sit at every second index, which is the step.

One moving group: `.hkcomb`, five marks and the four jumps between them. One
transform on it, `scaleX(2)` about the first mark, 900ms. The characters never
move, so the eye has one thing to follow and the jump getting longer IS the
argument. Nothing on this card is named: slice, step and method all arrive
later.

Geometry, so the transform can be written as arithmetic rather than guessed:

    cell i is centred at  C0 + i * CELL
    marks sit on cells 0, 1, 2, 3, 4   before the turn
    marks sit on cells 0, 2, 4, 6, 8   after it

    scaling by 2 about cell 0 sends c0 -> c0, c1 -> c2, c2 -> c4, c3 -> c6,
    c4 -> c8

`transform-origin` on an SVG group is not honoured everywhere, so the origin is
written into the transform: scaling by s about (a, 0) is
`translate(a * (1 - s), 0) scale(s, 1)`.

**Nothing else in the chat may contain the word.** Anjali's own messages would
