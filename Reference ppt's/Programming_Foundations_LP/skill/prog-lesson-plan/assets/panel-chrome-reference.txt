panel-chrome-reference.txt
==========================

The card treatments that got a plan past "horrible, very dull". Every one of
these was a rejection first. They are CSS and markup only; they ride the
template's own `data-anim` stepper and add no second engine.

Drop what you use into `EXTRA_CSS`. Nothing here needs the template edited.


1. A CARD THAT IS ONE PICTURE
-----------------------------
For a slide the plan keeps whole: a screenshot, a photograph, a fixed course
picture. The card is the picture, edge to edge, no padding and no caption.

  <div class="slide-img pic-card"><img class="pic-full" src="data:image/webp;base64,..." alt="..."></div>

  .pic-card{position:relative;aspect-ratio:16/9;overflow:hidden;border-radius:12px;background:#fff}
  .pic-card img.pic-full{position:absolute;inset:0;top:0;right:0;bottom:0;
   width:100%;height:100%;object-fit:cover;display:block}
  body.present .pic-card{border:0;border-radius:0}

`inset:0` is written out longhand as well, because the presenter is sometimes
opened in a browser old enough to ignore it and the picture then collapses to
nothing with no warning.


2. WIDE BODY TEXT SITS IN A SOFT CARD
-------------------------------------
A full-width paragraph across a card reads as a document, not a slide. The same
words inside a tinted card with an accent edge read as deliberate. This one
change is what a round of "the slides are dull" actually asked for.

  <div class="callout">The machine only knows how to make one copy.</div>

  .callout{background:#F2F8FB;border-left:6px solid var(--dk-cyan);
   border-radius:14px;padding:14px 20px;margin:10px 0}
  body.present .callout{padding:clamp(12px,1.8vh,22px) clamp(16px,1.9vw,30px);
   border-radius:clamp(10px,1.4vh,18px)}

Use it for one line, never for four. Four lines in a tinted box is a document
with a tint.


3. A CAPACITY IS A CLOSED BOX WITH A LID
----------------------------------------
Any bounded thing on screen - a list with a fixed size, a buffer, a room for N -
is drawn as a box with a lid and the words "room for N" on it, in every place it
appears: the panel, the reveal, and the worksheet column. A number in a sentence
does not stop anybody. A lid does.

  .lidbox{position:relative;border:2px solid var(--dk-blue);border-radius:10px}
  .lidbox::before{content:"room for " attr(data-cap);position:absolute;top:-11px;left:12px;
   background:#fff;padding:0 7px;font:600 12px var(--sans);color:var(--dk-blue)}

When something is pushed into a full box it bounces off the lid and the card
says why, in one short sentence. It never silently does nothing.

  @keyframes bump{0%,100%{transform:translateY(0)}35%{transform:translateY(-7px)}}
  .lidbox.full{animation:bump 260ms var(--ease-out)}


4. WHAT IS REMOVED, VANISHES
----------------------------
A value that came out of a structure is gone. It does not stay as a grey ghost,
because a ghost is exactly the misconception the beat exists to fix: the room
already thinks the old thing is still in there somewhere.

  .box.gone{animation:leave 220ms var(--ease-out) forwards}
  @keyframes leave{to{opacity:0;transform:translateY(-14px) scale(.92)}}

The one exception is a hook whose whole subject is history, where the pile of
what you left IS the point. Say so in the guide entry if you take it.


5. SMALL MOTIONS ON THE EXAMPLE CARDS
-------------------------------------
The "where else have you seen this?" cards each play one short motion, once, as
they are revealed: about two seconds, one moving element, in the spirit of the
hook and nowhere near its weight. A static grid of three examples is the beat
most often called flat.

  .ex-card .ex-fig{animation:none}
  .an-2 .ex-1 .ex-fig,.an-3 .ex-2 .ex-fig,.an-4 .ex-3 .ex-fig{
   animation:explay 2s var(--ease-in-out) 1 both}

Each card's motion starts on the press that reveals that card, not on load.


6. AN ANIMATION INSIDE A STEPPED CARD WAITS FOR ITS STEP
--------------------------------------------------------
A CSS `animation` on a figure inside a staged card runs the moment the card is
built. By the time the instructor presses to reveal it, it has already played
and the room sees the end state. Hold it:

  .stage[data-anim] .fig-anim{animation-play-state:paused}
  .stage[data-anim].an-4 .fig-anim{animation-play-state:running}

This is invisible in every screenshot and in every static check. The only way
to catch it is to step the card in a browser.


7. A REVEALED ANSWER REPLACES, IT DOES NOT STACK
------------------------------------------------
A revealed answer plus its Reveal row is about 110px on a 447px card. Four cards
that fit perfectly overflow the moment the answer appears, and the overflow
paints over the card's own title rather than scrolling. Make the answer take the
room the question was using:

  .stage-step:has(.ans.shown) .stage-note{display:none}   /* the timer is over */
  .stage-step:has(.ans.shown) .vopts{display:none}        /* they have voted */
  .stage-step:has(.ans.shown) svg.fig{max-height:9vh}     /* figures shrink, stay */

Measure every card twice, closed and revealed. The snippet is in
`references/gotchas.md`.
