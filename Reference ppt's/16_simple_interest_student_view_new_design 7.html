16 · Simple Interest

The one idea to landSimple interest is the same number of rupees every year, because the bank pays on the principal you put in and never on the amount the account has grown to. Amount = principal + interest, and the rate is never applied to the amount.

A shirt is marked ₹1,200. The shop gives a 25% discount. What is the selling price?

₹900After 25% off, 75% of the price remains: 1,200 × 0.75 = 900.
2

A shop gives 20% off, then another 10% off the reduced price. Is that the same as 30% off?

No. It is 28% off.The second discount is calculated on the reduced price.
3

An item marked ₹800 is sold for ₹680. What is the discount percent?

15%The discount is ₹120, and 120 ÷ 800 × 100 = 15%.

View statement

Day 1. Anu puts ₹5,000 in a fixed deposit at 10% a year.

Think. Use what you know about percentages. What is 10% of ₹5,000?

After one year. 10% of 5,000 is ₹500. The balance is ₹5,500.

This extra ₹500 is called interest.

When you invest money (put it in a bank)the bank pays you interest
When you borrow money (take a loan)you pay the bank interest
