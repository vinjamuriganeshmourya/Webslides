--- notes ---

1. Products initially failed to render and the page showed the empty-state
   illustration. Resolved on the user's side before capture. S1 is a genuine
   working state, not a partial one.

2. S2 shows `"count": 48` and a `next` page link. If a slide claims "the product
   list", note that the API is paginated and the first page is 12 of 48 items.
   Do not write copy that implies the endpoint returns everything.

3. `imageUrl` values point at `assets.ccbp.in`, a remote CDN. Harmless here, but
   worth knowing: the app is not fully self-contained, so a container that
   cannot reach the internet will render the grid without images. That is a
   Problem 2 concern and must not be opened in this session (hard rule 4).
